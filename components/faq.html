FAQ

Frequently asked questions about HomeSecure.

How do I arm or disarm my system?

Use the System Status toggle in the sidebar, or open Account Security and switch Armed Mode.

How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click Forgot password?, enter your email and a new password, then log in. If you are already signed in, go to Settings → Account Security → Change Password.

Why am I not receiving alerts?

Check that push notifications are enabled on your device and that your cameras are online under the Cameras view.

Can I add more users to my home?

Multi-user support is coming soon. For now, share your login credentials only with trusted household members.

Who do I contact for support?

Email support@homesecure.com or call our 24/7 helpline from your account welcome email.
